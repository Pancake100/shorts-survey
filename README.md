# Educational Short Video Impression Evaluation Experiment

This repository contains the experiment website developed for my undergraduate research on viewer impressions of educational short videos.

The study applies **Kansei Engineering** to investigate how viewers perceive educational short videos and which characteristics influence their overall evaluation. Participants watch a series of short educational videos covering different topics and evaluate each video using Semantic Differential (SD) scales based on Kansei adjective pairs.

The experiment focuses on three main dimensions:

- **Visual Presentation**
- **Content Quality**
- **Audio and Narration**

Participants also provide an **Overall Evaluation** for each video. The collected data are used to analyze the relationships between viewers' subjective impressions and their overall evaluations.

## Repository Structure

- `frontend/` – User interface for the experiment
- `backend/` – Server-side functions and data handling
- `analysis/` – Data processing and statistical analysis

## Research Purpose

The purpose of this research is to better understand how viewers perceive educational short videos and to identify important factors that contribute to positive viewer impressions. The findings may provide useful guidance for designing and producing more effective educational short-video content.
